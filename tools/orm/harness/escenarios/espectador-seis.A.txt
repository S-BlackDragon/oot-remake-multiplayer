# espectador-seis, A (ORM spectator camera C2, docs/ESPECTADOR_Y_JEFES.md "Resultado de C2"): six players, two games
# and four bots (P3-P6) walking a 4 m circle round A's start in Kokiri. A stands 7 s (the wake nudge comes 3 s after
# the camera is found), then life 0 and lies down; the d-pad goes round the five standing mates (right five times, then
# left once). B goes down at about 30 s (espectador-seis.B.txt): from then on the ring is the four bots, never P2.
# Run (from the repo root) with
#   python tools/orm/harness/r03_pareja.py --scenario espectador-seis --scen-dir tools/orm/harness/escenarios
#   --transport ws --bots 4 --bot-arg=-radius --bot-arg=4 --bot-arg=-center --bot-arg=-82,122,33.65
#   --env LICHII_COOP_DOWNED=1 --env LICHII_COOP_SPECTATOR=1 --env LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_CAMERA2=1 --capture-marks --timeout 480
