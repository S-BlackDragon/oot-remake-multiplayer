# ocarina-mano (ORM, issue 152, docs/OCARINA.md), B: the one who plays. Walks 1 m to the +z side of A's start, faces
# A, takes the Ocarina out (DLEFT, seen in daurosk's ocarina-find on 2026-11-11), plays the five notes slowly, then
# Zelda's Lullaby, and puts it away. The note mapping is daurosk's guess (ocarina-probe): A = A, L = down, R = right,
# Y = left, X = up. B's probe lines (LICHII_COOP_OCARINA_PROBE=1) say what our own AS slots play while the Ocarina is
# out, and which small words step per note: where the note is kept.
#! switches LICHII_COOP_PUPPET=1,LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_ANIM=1,LICHII_COOP_OCARINA_PROBE=1
#! expect B coop: puppets: the Ocarina in the hand while its player plays: sub weapon within boot..
#! expect B coop: ocarina probe: our Link plays the ocarina, \d+ AS slots
#! expect B coop: ocarina probe: slots 0=
#! expect B coop: ocarina probe: ocarina put away
#! saves 06-kakariko
mark start
wait 60
goto -82.0 34.7 0.4 600
toward -82.0 33.65 8         # face A
wait 300
# Round 5: in sondas8 and sondas12 B's control knew no song at all with the same 06-kakariko save ('known none',
# IsGet.Item_Melody_ZeldaLullaby read 0 at the play); in sondas9 it knew the Lullaby. Set here ('it read N' says
# which it was): this scenario is about the puppet's Ocarina and its sound, not the save.
flag IsGet.Item_Melody_ZeldaLullaby 1
mark ready
press DLEFT 12               # takes the Ocarina out
wait 240
# sondas7: the take-out may open a message first (EvtWait for 10 s, until an A press); an A here closes it, or else
# is a first note A. The ring the song check reads holds 8 notes (docs/SONDAS.md round 4).
press A 12
wait 120
mark out
wait 60                      # the Ocarina control is up 0.4 s after this (sondas4)
mark note-D
press L 12                   # D (the original's A button), read from the control in sondas4
wait 120
mark note-F
press R 12                   # F (C-down)
wait 120
mark note-A
press A 12                   # A (C-right): the button the four others leave
wait 120
mark note-B
press Y 12                   # B (C-left)
wait 120
mark note-highD
press X 12                   # high D (C-up)
wait 120
# sondas5: with the five notes and an A already in its 8-note ring, the game stopped taking notes at the lullaby's
# fourth (the ring full, no song) and never recognised it; sondas7, with fewer notes before it, did. So the
# Ocarina goes away and comes out again: the lullaby on an empty ring.
press B 12                   # puts it away
wait 120
press B 12
wait 180
mark again
press DLEFT 12               # out again
wait 300
mark lullaby                 # Zelda's Lullaby: B, high D, A, twice
press Y 12
wait 40
press X 12
wait 40
press A 12
wait 40
press Y 12
wait 40
press X 12
wait 40
press A 12
wait 40
wait 300                     # the song played back
mark after-song
press B 12                   # puts it away
wait 120
press B 12
wait 120
mark away
wait 300
mark end
