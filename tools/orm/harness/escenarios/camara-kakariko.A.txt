# camara-kakariko (ORM spectator camera, C1 step 5, docs/ESPECTADOR_Y_JEFES.md "Resultado de C1"): the camera chain
# away from Kokiri. One game loads the save straight into Kakariko, at the well (the test-only warp, as daurosk's
# warp.A.txt), then stands 10 s (the spectator's wake nudge comes 3 s after the camera is found), turns the camera
# with the right stick 0.5 s, and goes down (gate shut, life 0) for 5 s.
# Run with --players 1 --scen-dir tools/orm/harness/escenarios --env LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_CAMERA2=1
# --env LICHII_COOP_SPECTATOR=1 --env LICHII_COOP_DOWNED=1 --env LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_LIFE=1 --timeout 360.
# Expect: "coop: camera2: actor PlayerCamera", "camera ... by the known way +0xb8 -> +0x560 -> +0x730" (or by the
# walk) in KakarikoVillage_Child, the camera woken by the nudge, and the yaw word moving with the stick in step with
# the matrix heading (yaw - heading = 180).
#! noload
#! start coop: test warp done
mark start
warp KakarikoVillage_Child Kakariko_WellSide
wait 1200               # the title screen
mark title
press A
wait 180
press A
wait 180
press A
wait 300
press A                 # loads the save, then the warp
wait 2400               # the save loads, then the warp lands (about 30 s)
mark after
wait 600
mark turn
rstick 1 0 30
mark turned
wait 120
gate 1
life 0
mark downed
wait 300
mark end
