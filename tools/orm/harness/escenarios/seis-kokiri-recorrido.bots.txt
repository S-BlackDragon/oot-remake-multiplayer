# seis-kokiri-recorrido, the five bots (remake-bot -script, docs/BOTS.md). They join while A is at the title and follow it
# 3 m off into KokiriForest_Child and on through every scene A walks into; bot1 sends the block's flag; when A falls
# (r01-casa) bots 2 and 3 revive it, then bot1 goes down beside A for A to revive; bot5 goes away and back.
# Letterless directives read the bots' log; 'server' the server's.
#! transport ws
#! bot-arg -real -look auto
#! never script line \d+
#! never until P1 .*timed out
#! never revive: nobody down
#! count scene \S+ -> KokiriForest_Child >= 5
#! count scene KokiriForest_Child -> InsideTheDekuTree >= 5
#! expect bot1: flags: FLAG_SET sent \(0x502f6cea\[0\]
#! expect bot[23]: A held .* REVIVE sent to P1
#! expect bot1: alive -> downed
#! expect bot1: reviving -> alive: revived \(by P1\)
#! expect bot5: scene BotElsewhere -> \S+
#! count server player \d+ joined: bot\d >= 5
#! never server group defeat
#! never server refused a connection
scene BotLobby                              # not A's scene: following A in is a scene change
follow 1 3
until 1 scene KokiriForest_Child 400
wait 30
@1 flag 0x502f6cea bool 1                  # KokiriForest_Npc_Kokiri_Mido_Child_Blocker
until 1 down 1200                           # A's life 0 in r01-casa (mark caida)
@2-3 revive 1
until 1 up 120
@2-3 follow 1 3
@1 follow 1 1
@1 wait 8
@1 down                                     # A holds A beside it (mark levanta)
@1 wait 60                                  # the request, then up 6 s later (a revived bot stands)
@1 follow 1 3
@5 wait 20
@5 stand
@5 scene BotElsewhere 1                     # its puppet goes away in A
@5 wait 5
@5 follow 1 3                               # and following brings it back into A's scene
until 1 scene InsideTheDekuTree 1200
wait 1800                                   # following A to the end (the harness stops the bots)
mark done
