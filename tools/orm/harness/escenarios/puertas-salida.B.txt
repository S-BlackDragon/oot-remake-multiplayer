# puertas-salida (B), ORM passages (docs/PUERTAS.md): B waits in Kakariko 4 m from the Graveyard exit
# and watches A walk out and come back (puertas-salida.A.txt). Shots: w-out1..3 (every 0.5 s around A's exit: the
# puppet at the exit, then gone), w-in1..4 (A coming back: nobody mid-room, then A at the entrance walking out).
#! noload
#! start coop: test warp done
#! saves 06-kakariko
#! switches LICHII_COOP_PUPPET=1
#! timeout 480
#! expect B coop: puppet: P\d show -> hide \(left the scene\) within watching..end
#! expect B coop: puppet: P\d show -> hide \(arriving\) within watching..end
#! expect B coop: puppet: P\d hide -> show within watching..end
mark start
warp KakarikoVillage_Child Kakariko_GraveYardSide
wait 1200
mark title
press A
wait 180
press A
wait 180
press A
wait 300
press A
wait 2400
mark after
goto -6.0 71.0 1.0 300  # 4 m west of the start point, out of A's way
toward 16.7 64.1 8      # facing the exit
press ZL 10
mark watching
wait 1500               # A leaves about 25 s from here
mark w-out1
wait 30
mark w-out2
wait 30
mark w-out3
wait 1260               # A is in the Graveyard, then comes back
mark w-in1
wait 60
mark w-in2
wait 60
mark w-in3
wait 120
mark w-in4
wait 900
mark end
