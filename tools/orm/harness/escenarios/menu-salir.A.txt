# menu-salir (ORM, docs/MENUS_PAUSA.md M6 and M7), A: from the pause menu's System page A goes back to the title
# screen (ConfirmationBackToTitle), stays there 12 s, then loads its save again (Continue, as load.txt). A's log:
# "-> title" once no Link and no scene for 3 s, then "title -> playing" when its Link is back; never a menu save.
# B's log (menu-salir.B.txt): "salió al menú principal" and later "volvió a la partida" in the chat (not "entró":
# A was in the world before), A's PLAYER_STATUS title then playing.
# EXPLORATORY, the buttons: PLUS opens the menu and L x4 is its first page (menu-gear); the System page and the place
# of "Volver al título" on it are not known yet (MENUS_PAUSA.md "Falta ver en partida"): this guesses one L more
# (from the first page round to the last) and the last entry of the page, and each step has a capture mark
# (menu, system, asked, title) to fix the presses from. If the confirm is not reached the script closes nothing:
# the expects on 'title' fail and the captures say where it stopped.
# Run with --transport ws.
# Needs the hook of orm/menus2 (coop_menus.cpp, the paused hold in scene.hpp).
#! switches LICHII_COOP_PUPPET=1
#! timeout 900
#! expect A coop: menus: on \(LICHII_ORM_MENUS\) within boot..
#! expect A coop: menus: playing -> paused within start..asked
#! expect A coop: menus: (playing|paused) -> title within asked..reload
#! expect A coop: menus: title -> playing within reload..end
#! never A coop: menus: menu save seen
mark start
wait 300
press PLUS
wait 60
press L
wait 30
press L
wait 30
press L
wait 30
press L
wait 30
mark menu
press L                 # guess: round from the first page to the last, System
wait 45
mark system
press DUP               # guess: "Volver al título" is the page's last entry
wait 30
press A
wait 60
mark asked
press DLEFT             # guess: the confirm's "Sí" is on the left
wait 30
press A
wait 720                # the title screen: 3 s with no Link and no scene, then 9 s more
mark title
mark reload
press A
wait 180
press A
wait 180
press A
wait 300
press A                 # Continue: the save loads
wait 900
mark end
