A sets the room 10 step switch as if Link stood on it (FireTemple_Room10_StepSwitchOnLink: a held switch of the adult list, never shared plain), then opens the first Goron cell (Npc_Goron_001_ShutterisOpen, shared by the built-in list).

# --env LICHII_COOP_DUNGEON_LISTS=shared_dungeon_adult.txt (the adult branch's kit file, next to the emulator as

# lines of a flag only the adult list shares.
