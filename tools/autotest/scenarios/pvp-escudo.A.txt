# pvp-escudo, A (pvp-escudo.steps.txt): PvP on, next to B, three spins while B guards, turns, unequips.
# Each spin is held until B's window: B holds ZL for 600 ticks each time (pvp-escudo.B.txt).
#! expect A coop: pvp: on \(set by 1\)
#! expect A coop: pvp: our sword reached P2
#! expect A coop: pvp: P2 hurt by P1: 0 \(life \d+, flags 2\)
mark start
wait 120
chat /pvp on
wait 120
chat /ir B
wait 360
mark next-to-b
wait 240                # B: shield on, faces A, holds ZL
hold Y 70               # charge, let go: the spin
wait 90
mark spin-facing        # B: "blocked, takes 0; shield up (...), facing the hit"
wait 600                # B turns his back and holds ZL again
hold Y 70
wait 90
mark spin-behind        # B: "taken ...; shield up (...) but the hit came from beside or behind it"
wait 600                # B takes the shield off, faces A, holds ZL
hold Y 70
wait 90
mark spin-no-shield     # B: "taken ...; shield not up: no shield equipped"
chat /pvp off
wait 120
mark end
