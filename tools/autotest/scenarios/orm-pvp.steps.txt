# Both load in Kokiri. A goes next to B with /ir, tries a spin attack with PvP off (nothing),
# turns PvP on, spins again (B loses life and is pushed), B raises his shield toward A and A
# spins (blocked), then Z-targeting with "Fijar aliados" on and off, then both walk into
# each other (bodies). Read:
#  A: "coop: commands: /pvp", "coop: pvp: our hit #", "PvP activado por"
#  B: "coop: pvp: hit #1 from P1 (1 ...): taken", "coop: pvp: life 12 -> 8", "blocked", "PvP activado por"
#  both: "coop: pvp: out of P? 's body" while they walk into each other.
