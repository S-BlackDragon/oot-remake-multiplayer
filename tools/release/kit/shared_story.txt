# ORM story cutscenes for everyone (LICHII_ORM_STORY_EVENTS=1): the story events that play on
# every game in the same scene when one player's game starts them. One event a line, by
# the name the game gives it; HavePlayedEvent.<event> is always shared, and each flag after
# the name is shared with it too: the HavePlayedEvent.Dm_* of the timelines it plays, the
# Bools it sets at its end, and as step:Name=Value,... the story steps (Enum flags, every
# value in GameDataList order) it moves; a step only moves forward. The word solo after the name:
# only who starts it sees it (ZMP D-067: the cutscene after a blue warp is the warper's),
# its flags are shared all the same. near=M after the name: only the games whose Link is
# within M m of where its starter stood see it (ZMP D-188: a cutscene of one room of a
# scene, the trials of Ganon's Castle, is that room's); its flags are shared all the same.
#
# The whole game, child and adult, read from the romfs (oot-remake/tools/romfs: the
# EventSetting table names each event's flow and entry point, the flows say what it plays
# and moves, GameDataList has every name). Each comment says the flow and what its
# branches may play and move. Not listed: the boss rooms' intros and deaths, Ganondorf's and
# Ganon's too (the boss-room sync plays them; oot-remake docs/FINAL.md), dungeon mechanism cutscenes (Poe sisters, water levels,
# rooms that spin), minigames, the horse (each player's), Gerudo guards taking one Link to
# the cell (only that Link goes), chests and item gets, the boss rush.
