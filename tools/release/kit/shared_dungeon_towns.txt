# ORM towns with several players (oot-remake docs/PUEBLOS.md): Kakariko, the Graveyard, Lake
# Hylia, the Zora areas, Lon Lon, the Market and Hyrule Castle. Written by
# oot-remake/tools/romfs/town_list.py from the romfs (names checked against
# GameData/GameDataList: saved Bools; the keys are the adult towns' saved breakables, Banc
# placements with ResetTypeValue 24); edit the table there, not this file. A dungeon list file
# (coop/host dungeon_lists.hpp), loaded with LICHII_COOP_DUNGEON_LISTS after
# shared_dungeon_child.txt and shared_dungeon_adult.txt. NOT a LICHII_COOP_FLAGS file (that
# reader takes once: and hold: for names).
#
#   once:Name         done for good: a 1 is never undone by another game's 0 or by a load
#   hold:Name Place   shared, but held while our Link is in Place (either era, and a town's
#                     _Alternative version: KakarikoVillage_Adult_Alternative is Kakariko)
#   key64:0x...       a placement's one-time key (a 1 crosses, a 0 never undoes it)
#
# The rule (oot-64: one world for the group, D-061; ORM D-R12): what opens a way or is done
# for good in a town is done for everyone, once; what one player does there stays his own:
# his minigame run, his talks, the guard who caught him, the milk in his bottle, the letter
# in his bottle. Most of the towns' world state is already in other lists (PUEBLOS.md, 1).

# ---- Kakariko (child and adult). The Song of Storms drain, the well's ladder, the windmill door
# and the Death Mountain gate are the child list's and the story lists'; the windmill man's lesson
# is the song list's; Talon in Kakariko is the talk list's; the cucco lady's bottle is the minigame
# list's. Here: the ReDead grotto
once:KakarikoVillage_Gimmick_IsReDeadDestroyed    # the grotto's two ReDeads beaten (its chest): once for the group

# ---- Graveyard: the composer brothers' Poes and the Royal Tomb's first room. Dampe's digging tour
# is each player's (its heart piece is the minigame list's); the tomb opening, the Sun's Song and
# the Shadow Temple's door are the story lists' and the adult list's
once:GraveYard_Field_Sharp_Dead    # Sharp beaten: he comes back in no game
once:GraveYard_Field_Flat_Dead    # Flat beaten: he comes back in no game
once:RoyalFamilyTomb_Room00_Cleared    # the tomb's first room cleared: its locked shutter opens
hold:HavePlayedEvent.RoyalFamilyTomb_00_EnemyAnnihilation_Clear Graveyard    # its clear event: held while we are in the Graveyard

# ---- Lake Hylia (in Hyrule Field's scene). The water after the Water Temple is the story's (its
# step and timeline, built-in story list); its timeline flag is held here while we are in the field,
# so the lake never fills under a player who walks the dry bed (AGUA.md: nobody drowns from
# another's change). Ruto's letter is in the finder's bottle (bottle contents are each player's);
# the bottle in the lake goes for everyone
hold:HavePlayedEvent.Dm_LakeHrl_0020 HyruleField    # the lake's water: held while we are in Hyrule Field
once:Get_StoneOfWater_GetRutoLetter    # Ruto's letter found: its bottle is gone from the lake in every game

# ---- Zora's River, Domain and Fountain. King Zora stepping aside is the story's step (one time,
# for everyone); the frozen Domain thaws with the story's step. As adult the red ice on the King
# melts once for the world; the river's waterfall is the song list's
hold:SageOfWater_KingZora_RedIce_02 ZoraDomain    # the King's red ice (1 until melted): held while we are in the Domain
hold:HavePlayedEvent.SageOfWater_KingZora_IsMelted_Ice ZoraDomain    # the melting's event: held while we are in the Domain
once:Gimmick_MagicBeanSoil_ZoraRiver    # a magic bean planted in the river's soil: its plant grows in every game

# ---- Market and Hyrule Castle. The moat crates and Talon's waking are the child list's, Zelda,
# Impa and the lullaby the story lists'; a guard that catches a player throws out only him (oot-64
# D-210: each game's own). Lon Lon: Malon, Talon and Epona are the talk list's and D-R19's; a cow's
# milk goes in the player's own bottle
once:HrlCastle_GateOpen    # the castle's gate opened: open in every game
